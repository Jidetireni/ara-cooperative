# Ara Cooperative

This glossary distinguishes cooperative participation, account access, and financial activity.

## Language

**User**:
A person’s account for signing in and holding assigned roles.
_Avoid_: Member when referring only to account access.

**Admin**:
A user with management permissions for the cooperative. Admin access and cooperative membership are distinct concepts.

**Owner**:
The user who manages both the software and the cooperative, including admin access and fee settings.
_Avoid_: Admin when referring specifically to owner-only authority.

**Platform owner**:
The owner acting as the recipient of software maintenance-fee income. This is the same owner role, rather than a separate role.
_Avoid_: Admin when referring to the recipient of maintenance fees.

**Member**:
A cooperative participant with a membership profile and personal financial records.
_Avoid_: User when referring specifically to cooperative participation.

**Membership fee**:
The one-time fee associated with joining the cooperative. Existing code calls this the registration fee.
_Avoid_: Savings, share purchase.

**Maintenance fee**:
An additional charge payable to the platform owner, calculated as a percentage of the underlying contribution or repayment amount.
_Avoid_: Paystack processing charge.

**Paystack processing charge**:
The payment provider's charge for processing a payment.
_Avoid_: Maintenance fee.

**Savings deposit**:
A contribution recorded against a member’s personal savings.
_Avoid_: Membership fee.

**Campaign**:
A named cooperative event or project for which members contribute money.

**Special deposit**:
A member's contribution to cooperative funds designated for a campaign, recorded separately from personal savings.
_Avoid_: Savings deposit when referring to event or project contributions.

**Share purchase**:
A contribution recorded as units of a member’s shareholding.
_Avoid_: Savings deposit, membership fee.

**Loan application**:
A member's request for a loan that an admin reviews and decides.
_Avoid_: Approved loan before an admin makes an approval decision.

**Fine**:
A charge assigned to a member for a stated reason.
_Avoid_: Membership fee.

**Loan disbursement**:
The cooperative's payment of approved loan funds to a member.
_Avoid_: Savings withdrawal.

**Loan repayment**:
A member's payment toward their loan obligation.
_Avoid_: Loan disbursement.

**Flat-rate interest**:
Loan interest calculated using the original loan principal as its base.
_Avoid_: Reducing-balance interest.

**Reducing-balance interest**:
Loan interest calculated using the remaining unpaid principal as its base.
_Avoid_: Flat-rate interest.

**Main Paystack account**:
The cooperative's Paystack business account, whose payout account receives the contribution principal.

**Owner subaccount**:
The platform owner's bank account registered under the cooperative's Paystack business to receive maintenance-fee income.
